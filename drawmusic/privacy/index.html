2. Data that stays on your device

The App has no accounts or sign-in. The songs you draw, their names and your settings are stored only on your device and are never sent to our servers. All sounds are generated on your device. This data is deleted when you uninstall the App.

If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may back up this app data, encrypted, to your Google account. That backup is controlled by Google and we cannot access it. You can turn it off in your device settings under Google → Backup.

3. Sharing your songs

When you choose "Share as audio", the App creates an audio file of your song on your device and opens the Android share menu. The file goes only to the app or person you choose. We do not receive it.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the App shows ads through Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, and Google LLC). To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob may process:

  • Device identifiers (the Android advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Ad interactions (such as views and clicks)
  • Device, performance and crash information

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the App asks for your consent through Google's consent platform (UMP) before showing ads. Personalized ads are shown only with your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Without consent, non-personalized ads may be shown based on our legitimate interest in funding the free App (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings under Google → Ads. More information: How Google uses data.

If you have Pro, the App shows no ads.
